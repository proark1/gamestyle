# Stack or Sink: phone browser controls

The authorized mobile pass preserves the salvage-yard palette, Fredoka headlines, DM Sans labels, multiplayer rules and rigid-body placement. The primary job is to let two thumbs move, build and climb while the tower remains visible.

Use a compact height/flood strip, a five-action dock and separate Move/Jump controls, with both portrait and landscape arrangements. A scrollable menu is preferable to shrinking touch targets or requiring a particular orientation. Retain 44px controls, safe-area spacing and browser page zoom. Phone inputs use at least 16px text. Dialogs follow the visual viewport when the software keyboard opens, and long help starts at its heading.

Touch selects a surface and holds the preview in world coordinates. Place explicitly commits that preview. One finger orbits; two fingers pinch. Camera gestures never release cargo. Pointer ownership and cancellation prevent another finger, a dialog, rotation or an app switch from leaving movement stuck. Desktop mouse-click placement and keyboard controls continue to work.

The existing collection and Blend Business additions are preserved in the combined Railway release. Validate placement visually, gesture cancellation with unit tests, room behavior with four HTTP clients, and menus/dialogs/HUD at small portrait and landscape browser sizes. Browser-size testing does not constitute a physical iOS Safari or Android device playtest.
