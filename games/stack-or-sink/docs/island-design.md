# Stack or Sink island setting

The salvage crew is stranded on a small sandy island in open turquoise sea. An uneven sloping beach, rounded coastal rocks, sparse palms, a light rope boundary and three distant islets establish the setting. Fredoka/DM Sans controls, rounded characters, mustard machinery and existing salvage remain consistent with the collection. Sand `#e8d5a0`, wet sand `#bba77b`, palms `#50886a`, shallow water `#72c7ba` and open sea `#398c9b` define the environment.

The visible flat center covers the existing physical floor. The sloping beach is outside the established build boundary, which is marked by low posts and rope. Decorative trees and rocks remain outside player and salvage paths. No physics rules, flood timing or practice behavior change.

The water surface follows the authoritative flood height. Subtle directional ripples and a shallow-water color shelf give the sea depth. Broken foam bands trace the intersection of the current sea height with the beach and move inward as the water rises; they disappear once the flat island is submerged. Ambient motion stops for reduced-motion preferences. The overview camera fits the island in portrait and landscape.

Validation checks the terrain against the physical floor, shoreline progression, reduced-motion updates, the existing game tests, TypeScript and the production build. Public integration checks cover both games after publishing to the established Railway address.
