# Breathing, escape warning and angry wake-up

The sleeping giant's continuous shirt, chest details, loot and value-label groups use the same rendered breathing clock. This also works in the animated lobby, where physics does not advance. Loose treasure is seated against the visible mesh with clearance for its shape, including the raised knee and face details; its value label travels with its parent group. Carried or airborne loot keeps its normal motion. The necklace sits below the beard, clear of the collar buttons.

Feet point toward the ceiling while sleeping and pivot about their ankles as they plant for standing. Collision bounds follow the same foot transform. Tickling remains reachable at the raised feet. Angry inward-sloping brows, narrowed eyes and a moving open mouth accompany the wake-up dialogue.

The final warning begins twenty seconds before the actual scheduled wake time (the round deadline plus the existing three-second reaction). A noise-triggered early wake warns immediately during its shorter reaction. Shupia calls, an urgent spoken warning and a faster musical loop signal the escape. A visible countdown works when muted. The urgent score continues after he wakes, while snoring and the warning rattle stop.

On waking, the giant shouts: “What are you doing here? Why are you stealing my stuff? I will get you!” This line interrupts incidental commentary, lowers the music during speech, and also appears on screen. Playback uses a lower voice pitch. Both voice recordings were generated through the existing workshop with explicit user authorization and are bundled with the game. Existing workshop recordings, per-cue volumes and mixer settings take precedence. Snoring and warning sound defaults are original synthesized WAVs; regenerate them with `node games/dont-wake-the-giant/scripts/generate-sleep-audio.mjs`.

Regression checks cover the full breathing cycle against the rendered shirt surface, foot clearance through standing, the twenty-second boundary, early waking, reconnects, bundled assets and speech interruption.
