# Jumbleyard identity study — round three

The direction follows the restraint of [Meta Muse's new mark](https://daringfireball.net/misc/2026/09/muse.png): one deliberate gesture, one main color, and a compact app icon. The shapes here are original explorations for Jumbleyard. They avoid the earlier multicolor tiles, sparks, and stock game symbols.

The comparison fixes the wordmark to DM Sans and the primary mark color to cobalt `#245BE7`, so the symbol is the variable. Cobalt works against the site's pale cyan and green landing page. The three studies are:

1. **Interchange:** several paths cross and separate. Distinct in large uses, with fine details at small sizes.
2. **Four-way:** four players make one compact form. Strong at icon size, with a more abstract game connection.
3. **JY Gesture:** one continuous J/Y-inspired movement. The simplest and closest to the desired modern lettering feel.

These are generated raster concept studies, not final vector artwork. The comparison checks 32 px, 48 px, a site header context, and an app icon context. Once a direction is chosen, redraw and optically correct the selected mark as SVG before replacing live branding.

## Generation

Built-in ImageGen was used with transparent backgrounds. Each prompt asked for a single flat cobalt mark with generous clear space, clean edges, strong small-size recognition, and no wordmark, tile, gradient, shadow, gamepad, die, smiley, or imitation of Meta Muse. The specific forms requested were:

- A continuous ribbon suggesting player paths meeting and springing apart.
- Four rounded forms tumbling into a single coherent silhouette.
- A gestural fused J/Y monogram with a rebound in its stroke.

The transparent PNGs are saved alongside `compare.html` and `compare.png`.
