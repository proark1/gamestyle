# Four-player color explorations for logo 03

[Open the comparison board](compare.png). The approved J/Y silhouette stays intact in every direction. The concept palette draws from the current landing artwork: water blue `#2B6D9A`, sea teal `#258F96`, clay coral `#D95D49`, and prop gold `#E6A243`.

1. **Color in the gesture:** four branches of the symbol carry the four colors; the wordmark stays Night Ink. This is the clearest small icon.
2. **Color in the name:** the symbol stays one color; four word groups bring a Mario-like rhythm to the lettering.
3. **Color everywhere:** four colors appear in the symbol and repeat letter by letter in the name, with tiny baseline shifts. This is the most chaotic treatment.

The gold used in large wordmark previews is darker (`#A9792D`) so it stays visible on the pale sky background. The brighter gold remains in the symbol. These are comparison concepts; the single-color logo assets in `public/images/brand/` remain the current site identity until a color treatment is selected.
