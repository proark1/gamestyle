# Cage Clash fight camera and floor exchanges

Cage Clash keeps its readable clay art direction while making punches, ground positions and reactions easier to follow. A local View slider and mouse wheel move from arena framing to a close view; full zoom follows the local fighter's viewpoint while standing. Floor fighting uses a tight elevated angle so both bodies, hands, and the mat remain visible without camera clipping. It returns to arena framing between rounds. Camera choice is local and does not change peer state.

On the ground, hands have distinct placements for defense, posting, strikes and submissions. Top punches alternate hands; an advancing top fighter moves forward visibly. The lower fighter twists while reversing and arches during a bridge. Hit feedback stays near the bodies instead of floating high above the mat.

The lower fighter can tap Shift + Q while guarding to bridge. The burst costs 18 stamina, moves positional progress toward escape by 0.48 from mount or 0.38 from guard, and has a 0.9-second cooldown. Holding the keys cannot repeat it. The status panel shows readiness and the action is documented in help. Since the rule affects multiplayer, Cage Clash's rules version increments to 3; other games are unchanged.
